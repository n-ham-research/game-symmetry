\begin{abstract}
We revisit definitions of symmetry in finite strategic-form games, separating
invariance under player permutations from invariance under strategy relabeling
and from equality of realized payoffs. We state consistent permutation and
isomorphism conventions and review a known characterization of the permutation
condition associated with Dasgupta and Maskin: for at least three players it is
equivalent to total symmetry together with identical payoff functions.
A short proof and boundary examples make explicit that payoffs may vary across
profiles. This initial draft records established foundations; the full
classification, historical verification and proposed fairness extensions remain
a research programme. No new theorem is claimed.
\end{abstract}
