# R-P3-FAIR-LIT-B — primary fairness literature reconstruction

Internal research role, supplied the two new user steering attachments and
assigned VNM, Kohlberg–Neyman, and Dimitrov–Sun. I read the cached VNM third
edition whole Section 28 in OCR, visually checked its six printed pages
255–260, and checked the coalition definition and fixed-payment context in
OCR. I read publisher full-body extracts of KN21 §§1–4 and proof conclusion,
and DS25 §§2–4 including both theorem statements and independence examples.
This exceeds abstract/heading inspection, but does not constitute independent
verification of every modern-paper proof. PDF screenshot cache failures and
version distinctions are recorded in the research note.

Key findings:

- VNM's sufficient group condition is set-transitivity, not merely individual
  player transitivity; fairness is coalition-size dependence of the zero-sum
  characteristic function. Their own warning excludes an inference of equal
  individual realized outcomes for n>=3. The historical scan is third edition,
  not newly verified 1944 text.
- KN21's precise premise additionally requires identical focal payoff functions
  and preservation of the entire payoff vector under the strategy swap. It
  axiomatizes an allocation rule under transferable utility, not Nash play.
- DS25's cooperation-equivalence condition covers every coalition intersecting
  the nonfocal players; only the focal singleton and empty set are exempt.
  Blend-in and rule anonymity are independent, and the equal-division theorem
  has explicit superadditive/cohesive scope restrictions.
- Independently derived: fixed translations preserving zero sum can change
  VNM fairness without changing cardinal preference data. This prevents an
  unqualified identification with cardinal-automorphism transitivity.
