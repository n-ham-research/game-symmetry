\section{Classification: verified starting points}
In the common-label setting total symmetry implies player transitivity and
opponent anonymity. Conversely, assume both properties and fix $\pi,i$.
Choose a symmetry $\tau$ with $\tau(i)=\pi(i)$. Then
$h=\tau^{-1}\pi$ fixes $i$, so
\[
 u_i=u_i\circ P_h
     =(u_{\tau(i)}\circ P_\tau)\circ P_h
     =u_{\pi(i)}\circ P_\pi.
\]
Thus the game is totally symmetric. This is the central equivalence of
Ham's Theorem~3.18 \citep{ham2021}.

The label-independent hierarchy requires additional care: transitivity
of the player image allows strategy relabeling, whereas label-preserving
transitivity uses only the maps $P_\pi$. A complete implication diagram and
its separating examples will be included only after the matching constructions
and parameterized examples in Ham's Sections~4--5 have been audited.
