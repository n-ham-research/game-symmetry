\section{Conclusion}
The inverse-permutation condition has a precise common-payoff characterization
for at least three players. Equal payoffs within each profile do not require
constant payoffs across profiles, and conventional symmetry permits unequal
realized payoffs. Consistent action and isomorphism conventions supply a
foundation for the remaining audit. Historical priority, full classification
and possible fairness extensions require further verification before a
submission manuscript can be prepared.
