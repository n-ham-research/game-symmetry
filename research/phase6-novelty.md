# Phase 6 targeted novelty assessment

This is a comparison record, not priority clearance. The prior source audit
in phase5-novelty-comparison.md remains applicable.

The inspected primary Lassueur–Mazza author manuscript (18May2015), §3,
recalls that the alternating-group Schur multiplier has order2 for n≥8 and
order6 at n6,7. Source:
https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/82264/1/endoSchurAnSn_Revised.pdf .
The underlying multiplier theorem and universal-central-extension facts are
literature. Combining them with the audited residual-extension reduction
excludes all other player counts. The new only-six game corollary is a
candidate application, not a new classification of Schur multipliers.

Targeted search used the combinations: game/symmetry/nonsplit/six;
game/automorphism/3.S6; ternary/wreath/complement/symmetric group;
common-interest/automorphism/group realization. Results did not produce an
inspected theorem equivalent to our complete player-count statement. That
search outcome is not evidence of first discovery. Mathematical papers may
express the same claim through block systems or relational structures.

The important comparison is structural: a code C⊆A^n is exactly a winning
relation for the common-interest binary game u_i=1_C. Its typed game group is
the code's coordinate/symbol automorphism group. This elementary encoding
is not itself a new realization theorem. The known Hadamard/triple-cover
representation may therefore make the bare game construction a translation
of prior coding/design mathematics. The claims needing priority scrutiny are
the precise standardness and hierarchy application, actual-payoff restrictions,
classification and sharp/unique player-count result, not the group name or
indicator encoding.

Cao–Li–Tan–Yang2024 provides prior covering closure and payoff realization;
Gillespie–ÓCatháin–Praeger2018 and ATLAS provide prior3.S6/18point data.
The original Ham2021 Figure4/binary classification remains prior. Existing
final Cao–Yang2018, Dasgupta–Maskin proof and final Plan body gaps are retained;
their repeated retrieval is not part of this pass. A full coding/design and
permutation-representation literature comparison by a specialist remains a
publication-readiness requirement. No absence claim is inferred from terms
or titles differing from ours.
