For a later direct comparison, please supply readable copies or page images of:

1. **Dasgupta–Maskin1986, PartI, Review of Economic Studies53(1),1–26:**
   Definition 7 with surrounding conventions; Lemmas6–7 with their complete
   proofs; Theorems6 and6* including every referenced appendix argument.
   A complete readable article is preferable to disconnected displays because
   the requested question is proof dependency, not just definition wording.
2. **Plan, *Symmetry in n-player games*, JET207(2023),105549:** the final body,
   especially its symmetry definitions, main proofs and passages discussing
   Dasgupta–Maskin. The accessible author PDF is still a separately dated2017
   draft; no final-versus-draft equivalence is assumed.
3. **Cao–Yang, *Symmetric games revisited*, MSS95(2018),9–18:** the final body,
   including definitions of ordinary/renaming/name-irrelevant symmetry,
   Theorem 3's potential argument and the group characterization. The
   July29,2015 author draft is readable and inspected, but not a substitute
   for the revised final article.
4. **Cheng–Liu2018, *From Boolean game to potential game*, Automatica96,51–60**
   and **Wang–Li–Feng2024, *On potential equations of finite symmetric games*,
   AJC26,2367–2376:** full symmetry definitions and binary/potential theorems
   with proofs. Current preview material identifies relevant claims but does
   not clear their full implications.
5. **VNM1944 first edition:** title/copyright pages and the whole Section 28,
   with the table of contents if pagination differs. The inspected third
   edition supports its own equations/wording, not a claim of first-edition
   textual identity. The Library Genesis search lead and restricted catalogue
   records did not supply verified first-edition pages.

These are source gaps, not impediments to the independent finite-game proofs.
The repository retains their status explicitly and does not ask the user to
approve or resolve already authorized mathematical work.
