- §2 maximin/minimax values: opponents mix independently; compare coalition-correlated formulation separately.
- §2 strictly increasing maps/positive affine maps: see counterexample U2-M; surjectivity missing.
- §2 group actions, transitive/regular/n-transitive, groupoid, coset: ensure n-transitive means full player image, not n-transitivity of a nonfaithful action on strategies.
- §3 actions on focal mixed strategies and opponent profiles: source/target typed by the moved player.
- §4 S_Gamma, Aut(Gamma), G_N, arrow G: keep all four objects distinct.
- §4 matching maps M_ij and induced lifts M_pi: coherence is required.
- §4 ordinal/cardinal isomorphisms: pure-order versus mixed-utility equivalence must be distinguished.
