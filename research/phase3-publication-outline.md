# Preliminary publication narrative after Phase 3

16 October 2026. A planning document conditional on author scrutiny, literature
comparison and external review. No publication or priority claim is made.

## Best current main-paper candidate

Working theme: **Player interchangeability, lifting obstructions, and binary
potential games.** The central question is which player permutations can be
implemented, whether implementations can be chosen coherently, and what the
actual action on binary strategies forces about incentives.

1. Motivate individual realizability versus coherent role exchange. Fix typed
   strategy maps and the specified projection q at the outset. Introduce only
   the audited symmetry/matching facts needed by the proofs.
2. Separate three properties: q onto S_n; a section of q; a strategy-trivial
   section. Cite Ham's already published distinction between the latter two,
   rather than presenting it as new. Explain standardness as a separate
   transitive strategy-trivial witness.
3. Settle the 2021 splitting converse negatively with the explicit three-player,
   four-strategy, two-payoff game. Prove its **full** automorphism group and the
   transposition-order obstruction. State only proved minimalities: player
   count3 and payoff alphabet2; four-action minimality remains open.
4. Present binary rigidity and the exact-potential theorem. The extension is
   unusually constrained by four possible binary kernels. Explain the
   three-player signed exception and the four-player no-incentive exception.
   Prove the unbounded-player statement algebraically; use finite computations
   only as reproducible checks and illustrations.
5. Derive the original binary pure-Nash answer and stronger conclusions. Make
   clear which fully symmetric potential/existence facts are prior literature.
   Give matching pennies and directed odd cycles to show why assumptions matter.
6. Conclude with one carefully specified assignment experiment showing why
   coherent interchangeability has operational meaning. Do not expand this
   into an unproved general theory of fairness.

An appendix can hold exact payoff tables, compact algorithms, the bitorsor
observation if used for lift choices, and necessary corrections to earlier
formulations. The full 41-result audit stays in the repository. A corrected
classification paper should not bury the new algebraic argument in a long
catalogue of old definitions, and the new paper should not reproduce the
2021 manuscript with appended errata.

## What to keep separate

The broader fairness programme is a plausible second paper only after an
independent assignment/information/utility model is philosophically justified
and compared with decision-theoretic literature. Current operational lemmas
and separating examples are useful preparation, not yet a comprehensive
normative hierarchy. Coalition values require a separately justified
general-sum construction. Keep their present limitations explicit.

An eventual corrected survey or short correction note is another possibility,
but corrections alone need not determine the main new-result narrative. The
four-player/six-strategy construction is a useful alternate obstruction in
research notes; the smaller witness should lead the paper.
