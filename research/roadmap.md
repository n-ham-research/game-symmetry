# Game Symmetry publication roadmap

After Phase6,16October2026. The structural manuscript is a self-contained
research draft; utility/fairness maintenance is canonical in game-fairness.
Read handover-phase-6.md and publication-readiness.md.

1. Seek human mathematical criticism of the actual-group hypothesis, ternary
   finite lemma, six-player standard subgroup/perfectness certificates, and
   the new only-six corollary importing classical multiplier theory.
2. Complete specialist priority comparison with code/design automorphism
   groups and block systems. Encoding a code as a binary common-interest
   game is immediate and is not itself the novelty claim.
3. Investigate represented-action uniqueness of the six-player obstruction,
   minimal exact realizations and stronger reduction systems. This is more
   significant than increasing enumeration limits.
4. Seek a conceptual proof replacing the32/11case ternary lemma. Preserve
   the existing independent implementations and exact domain specifications.
5. Retain binary exact-potential/rigidity research and final-source requests
   here. It need not be a main section of this lifting paper and must not be
   prematurely moved to the fairness manuscript.

Settled internally: three-player ternary mechanisms6/18; standard ternary
splitting at every player count except6; explicit binary common-interest
six-player counterexample. At n≥5,n≠6 full actual player image alone suffices.

closed by the source-based multiplier corollary, not new enumeration.
Novelty and external review remain outstanding. No submission is authorized.
